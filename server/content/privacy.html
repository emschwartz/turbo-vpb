TurboVPB

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

TurboVPB sends the contact shown on your phone bank page to your phone. Those contact details are encrypted end to end: the key is created in your browser and never sent to the server, so only your computer and your phone can read them.

The TurboVPB server relays the encrypted messages and keeps anonymous counts of calls and texts, listed below. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts, and no selling of data.

What the extension reads and keeps on your computer

The extension only runs on phone bank pages: OpenVPB, VAN (VoteBuilder and EveryAction, including campaign-specific VAN domains), and BlueVote. It also runs on turbovpb.com to import message templates. It does not read any other site.

On a phone bank page it reads the contact the page is currently showing: their name, phone number, and the other details the page displays. It sends that contact to your phone, encrypted, and does not store it anywhere.

The extension keeps these settings in your browser's extension storage, on your computer only:

  • Your text message templates and the name you enter for them.
  • A custom server URL, if you set one.
  • Your call counts (total and per day), which the extension popup shows you.
  • For each open phone bank tab, the pairing with your phone (a random channel id and the encryption key) and which call results have already been handled. Your browser clears these when it closes.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this.

What passes through the relay server

When you scan the QR code, your computer and your phone join a channel on the TurboVPB server with a random id. Every message between them is encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM. The key is in the part of the link after the # (the URL fragment), which browsers never send to a server.

The server sees the channel id, whether each side is connected, and the encrypted messages, including their size and timing. It cannot read them.

The server holds the most recent encrypted message from each side in memory, so it can deliver it again when your phone reconnects (your phone's browser drops the connection every time you switch to the dialer or messaging app). Nothing is written to disk. A connection that is idle for 30 minutes is closed, and as soon as the last connection to a channel closes, the channel and the messages it held are dropped.

The link in the QR code also carries a random session id, the extension version, the phone bank site's domain, and your desktop browser's user agent string. The server receives these when the page loads. It stores the first three only as part of the usage statistics below, and does not store the user agent.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see how much TurboVPB is used and whether an update broke something, the page on your phone sends a small record to the server after each call and each text. The records contain exactly these fields:

  • Each call: a random session id (new each time you pair), how long the call lasted, the result code you picked (for example "Not Home"), the extension version, the phone bank site's domain (for example www.openvpb.com), and the time.
  • Each text: the session id, the extension version, the phone bank site's domain, and the time.

The records never include names, phone numbers, or any other contact details, and no IP addresses or browser details. The session id is random and is not linked to you, your campaign, or an account.

These records are kept in a database on the server. There is currently no automatic deletion, so they are kept indefinitely.

The server also keeps running totals (such as the number of open channels and messages relayed) for monitoring. These are aggregate counters with no per-user information.

The website

Like any web server, the TurboVPB server receives your IP address and standard browser headers with each request, including from the page on your phone. The server does not log or store them.

TurboVPB sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. The page on your phone loads nothing from third parties. The home page embeds videos from YouTube (through youtube-nocookie.com): if you play one, YouTube receives your request and Google's privacy policy applies to it.

Hosting

The server runs on Fly.io, which processes data on TurboVPB's behalf. Fly.io's network necessarily sees the IP addresses of connections to the server and may keep its own infrastructure logs, which TurboVPB does not control. See Fly.io's privacy policy.

No one else receives any data from TurboVPB.

What TurboVPB does not collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email addresses (unless you email us).
  • No contact details on the server. They only ever exist unencrypted on your own devices.
  • No browsing history. The extension does not look at pages outside the phone bank sites listed above.
  • No IP addresses or user agents stored by TurboVPB.
  • No advertising, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of data for marketing.

Chrome Web Store

TurboVPB uses the information it reads from phone bank pages only to send the current contact to your phone and to show you your call counts. TurboVPB complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Self-hosted servers

This policy covers the server at turbovpb.com. If you enter a custom server URL in the extension's options, your relay traffic and usage statistics go to that server instead, and whoever runs it is responsible for them.

Open source

The extension and the server are open source, so you can check everything on this page against the code: github.com/emschwartz/turbo-vpb.

Your choices and rights

Everything the extension keeps is on your own devices, and you can delete it by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser data. The usage statistics on the server are tied only to a random session id, so there is no record that TurboVPB can look up or delete for a particular person.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: TurboVPB is operated by Evan Schwartz, who is the data controller. The legal basis for relaying messages and keeping usage statistics is a legitimate interest in running and improving the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. The server is in the United States.

If you are in California: TurboVPB does not sell or share personal information.
